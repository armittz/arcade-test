Cat and Laser
Who plays: One player.
On screen: A cat explores a room filled with furniture, toys, and distractions. Move the mouse and hold the left mouse button to shine a laser that the cat follows. Guide it around furniture, keep the dot within sight, and bring its attention back when yarn or wind-up mice distract it.
Win or lose: Collect three toys and lead the cat to its bed within 60 seconds. Complete five rooms to win. Lose if time runs out in any room.
