Rocket Explorer —
Who plays: One player, piloting a rocket through space.
On screen: Use the arrow keys or WASD to fly your rocket around the screen while space scrolls past you. Dodge spinning asteroids, passing spaceships, and enemy ships that aim energy blasts at your position. Collect fuel cells to keep flying and star crystals for points. A warning appears before large hazards enter the screen.
Each journey leads to a different planet: a moon with giant craters, a crystal planet, a volcanic world, an icy planet, and a planet surrounded by storms. When you arrive, fly freely above its surface to explore and collect three discovery beacons before landing on a marked pad. Each planet introduces its own challenge, such as erupting lava, drifting ice chunks, or wind that pushes your rocket sideways. Later journeys have faster hazards and spaceships approaching from more directions. Give each planet a distinct appearance, with glowing landscapes and colorful space backgrounds.
Win or lose: Explore and land on all five planets to win. Your rocket has three shield points; hitting an asteroid, spaceship, energy blast, or surface hazard removes one. Fuel drains while flying, but collectible fuel cells refill it, and landing restores your fuel and one shield point. Lose if your shields or fuel reach zero. Earn points for discoveries, star crystals, and completing journeys without damage, and try to beat your saved highest score.
