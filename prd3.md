On screen: A customer calls, and their character briefly shows a picture of the pizza they want before it disappears. Recreate it by clicking ingredients, placing toppings, and clicking Bake. Each level introduces more complicated topping patterns, quantities, and half-and-half combinations. Show your character boxing the pizza, carrying it to the car, and loading it inside. Drive with the arrow keys through a colorful town with other cars moving through streets and intersections. Use a small map to find the customer; later addresses require longer routes, more turns, and detours. Park outside the correct house and press Space to show your character carrying the box to the door. The customer opens it and reacts with expressions and animations. Use pictures, icons, meters, and short speech bubbles to explain what is happening.
Win or lose: Complete eight orders with at least five satisfied customers to win. Customers are satisfied when pizza accuracy is at least 80%, warmth is at least 30%, and condition is at least 50%. Driving takes time and cools the pizza; crashes damage it. Three dissatisfied customers end the shift. Earn money and tips for accurate, hot, undamaged deliveries, and beat your saved highest score.
